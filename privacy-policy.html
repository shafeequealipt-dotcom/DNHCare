Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or book a consultation, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible for your information

Dr. Nafia's Homoeopathic Medical Centre (DNH Care), Opp. KK School, Gunjur Main Road, Varthur, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560087, decides how your information is used and is responsible for it. This policy covers dnhcare.co.in and our booking site, book.dnhcare.co.in.

What we collect

When you book a consultation:

  • The patient's name, age and mobile (WhatsApp) number.
  • Your language, how you want to consult (clinic or WhatsApp video call), and the time you chose.
  • Your answers to our symptom questions, such as how long the pain has lasted and what treatment you have tried. This is health information.
  • Whether your payment succeeded, the amount, and the payment reference. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login. Razorpay handles those.
  • Your consent to be contacted and to the use of your details as described here.

Technical information: your IP address, browser type, and, if you arrive from one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, the ad click identifier and campaign name. Our main website does not use advertising or analytics trackers. It uses Google Fonts to display text.

Why we use it

  • To arrange and carry out your consultation, and to prepare Dr. Nafia for it.
  • To confirm, remind you about and reschedule your booking, including by WhatsApp message or phone call.
  • To take payment, issue refunds and keep records.
  • To measure whether our advertising reaches the people it is meant for.
  • To keep our systems secure and to meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with services that help us run the clinic:

  • Razorpay, to process payments and refunds.
  • Meta (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram): we may send you a booking confirmation through WhatsApp. If you consent when booking, we also send a scrambled (hashed) version of your phone number to Meta so we can tell whether our ads led to bookings. Your health answers are never sent to Meta.
  • Cloud hosting and messaging tools that we use to store bookings and to alert clinic staff about new bookings (for example, an internal Telegram alert).
  • Authorities or courts, only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and consultation-preparation records for as long as needed to provide your care and meet legal and medical record-keeping duties. We do not keep information longer than we need it.

Keeping it safe

Our booking site uses HTTPS. Bookings are stored on a private server, and access is limited to clinic staff. No online system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

Under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • stop contacting you, or withdraw a consent you gave earlier.

To do any of this, or to raise a concern about how your information is handled, call or WhatsApp the clinic using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Consultations for children must be booked by a parent or legal guardian, who agrees to this policy on the child's behalf.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
