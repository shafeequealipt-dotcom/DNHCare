Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective 26 October 2026. This policy explains how to change a booking and when you get your consultation fee back.

Consultation fee

The consultation fee is ₹300, paid online when you book (clinic visit or WhatsApp video call).

Rescheduling

You can move your booking to another time free of charge up to 4 hours before your booked time. Call or WhatsApp the clinic on +91 96637 79358 and we will find you a new time.

When you get a refund

  • The clinic cancels or cannot see you: a full refund, or a new time if you prefer.
  • Your payment was taken but the booking was not confirmed (for example, the time was given to someone else while your payment was going through): a full refund, or a new time if you prefer.
  • You were charged more than once for the same booking: the extra amount is refunded.

When a refund is not given

If you cancel less than 4 hours before your booked time, or you do not attend or cannot be reached at the booked time, the fee is not refundable. We are always willing to discuss genuine emergencies. Please contact the clinic.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. They normally reach your account within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank or UPI app.

If money left your account but the payment failed

Money deducted for a failed payment is usually returned automatically by your bank within 5–7 working days. If it does not reach you, call us with the date, amount and payment reference and we will help trace it.

How to ask for a refund or change

Call or WhatsApp the clinic with the patient's name and the mobile number used for the booking.
