Understanding Fungal Nail Infections and Their Prevention

Fungal nail infections, also known as onychomycosis, are common infections that can affect both fingernails and toenails. They are usually caused by a type of fungus that infects the nail bed. In Bengaluru's climate, especially during the cooler months, the risk of fungal nail infections may increase due to increased moisture. Prevention is key, and maintaining good hygiene, keeping nails short, and avoiding sharing personal care items can help reduce the risk. Homeopathic approaches focus on supporting the body's natural defenses and may include guidance on diet, lifestyle, and an individually selected remedy to support nail health.

  • Wear shoes that breathe and change your socks regularly
  • Avoid sharing nail care tools
