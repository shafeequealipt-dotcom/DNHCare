Understanding Monsoon-Triggered Migraines in Bengaluru

The monsoon season in Bengaluru can bring a mix of relief from the summer heat and frustration from the unpredictable weather. For some individuals, the changing weather patterns can trigger migraine attacks, which can be debilitating and affect daily life. Research suggests that people with sensory processing sensitivity are more prone to weather-sensitive migraines, making it essential to explore effective management strategies.

  • Changes in atmospheric pressure
  • Humidity and temperature fluctuations
  • Increased exposure to mold and pollen
