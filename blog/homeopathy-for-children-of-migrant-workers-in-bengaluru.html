Understanding Separation Anxiety in Children of Migrant Workers

Separation anxiety is a common issue among children of migrant workers, particularly during the monsoon season when parents may be away from home for extended periods. This can lead to feelings of uncertainty, fear, and anxiety in children, affecting their emotional and mental well-being. Homeopathic treatment can help address these issues by promoting relaxation and reducing anxiety.

In Bengaluru, many migrant workers face challenges in maintaining a stable family life due to their work commitments. This can result in children experiencing emotional distress, which may manifest as separation anxiety, bedwetting, or irritability. Homeopathic approaches can help children develop coping mechanisms and build resilience to deal with these challenges.
