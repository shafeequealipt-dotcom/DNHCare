Understanding the Risks of Monsoon Pesticide Exposure for Kids' Neurodevelopment

The monsoon season in Bengaluru brings relief from the heat but also increases the use of pesticides in homes and public spaces. This surge in pesticide use poses significant risks to children's neurodevelopment, as their brains are more susceptible to the toxic effects of these chemicals. Research has shown that exposure to pesticides during critical periods of development can lead to cognitive, behavioral, and motor skill impairments.

Homeopathic approaches recognize the importance of minimizing exposure to harmful substances, especially during the monsoon season. By adopting safe pest control measures and using homeopathic remedies tailored to each child's symptom pattern, parents can support their children's health and neurodevelopment.

  • Use natural pest control methods like citronella, lemongrass, or neem oil
  • Seal all food and waste properly to avoid attracting pests
  • Wear protective clothing when applying pesticides
