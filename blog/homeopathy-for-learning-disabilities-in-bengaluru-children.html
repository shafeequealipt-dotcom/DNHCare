Understanding Learning Disabilities and Monsoon-Related Challenges

Learning disabilities such as dyslexia, dysgraphia, and dyscalculia can affect a child's ability to learn and succeed in school. The monsoon season in Bengaluru can exacerbate these challenges, with increased humidity and rain leading to decreased outdoor playtime and increased screen time. This can result in a range of issues, including decreased attention span, increased frustration, and decreased motivation.

  • Increased humidity can lead to decreased focus and attention span
  • Reduced outdoor playtime can result in increased screen time and decreased physical activity
  • Monsoon-related stress can exacerbate existing learning disabilities
