Understanding the Skin-Gut Axis and Its Impact on Monsoon Skin Issues

The skin and gut are closely linked, with an imbalance in the gut microbiome potentially leading to skin issues such as acne, eczema, and psoriasis. During the monsoon season, the humid climate in Bengaluru can exacerbate these issues, making it essential to address both skin and gut health. Homeopathic treatment for skin issues in Bengaluru takes into account the individual's overall health, including their gut health, to provide a comprehensive approach to skin care.
