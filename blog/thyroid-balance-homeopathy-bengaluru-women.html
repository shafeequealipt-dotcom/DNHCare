Understanding Monsoon-Related Thyroid Changes in Women

The monsoon season in Bengaluru can be particularly challenging for women with thyroid issues. The humid climate and changes in temperature can disrupt thyroid function, leading to fluctuations in TSH levels. This, in turn, can cause a range of symptoms, including fatigue, weight gain, and mood swings. Homeopathy can help alleviate these symptoms by addressing the underlying hormonal imbalances and supporting the immune system.
