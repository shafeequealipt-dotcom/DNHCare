Understanding Migraine Triggers and Symptoms

Migraines are complex neurological conditions that can be triggered by various factors, including loud noises, certain foods, and environmental changes. The symptoms can range from debilitating headaches to sensitivity to light and sound, and in some cases, ringing in the ears, also known as tinnitus. Homeopathic treatment for migraine aims to address these symptoms by identifying and treating the underlying causes, rather than just the symptoms themselves.
