Understanding Sinusitis and Nasal Polyps

Sinusitis is an inflammation of the sinuses, often triggered by allergies, colds, or poor air quality. Nasal polyps are growths that can occur in the nasal passages, causing congestion and discomfort. Both conditions can significantly impact daily life, especially during periods of high pollution like the festival season in Bengaluru.

  • Symptoms of sinusitis include nasal congestion, headache, and facial pain
  • Nasal polyps can cause loss of smell, congestion, and difficulty breathing
