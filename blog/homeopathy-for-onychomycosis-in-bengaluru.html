Understanding Onychomycosis and Its Impact on Nail Health

Onychomycosis is a common fungal infection that affects the nails, causing discoloration, thickening, and brittleness. In Bengaluru's humid climate, the risk of developing onychomycosis increases, particularly during the monsoon season. Homeopathic treatment may help support the body's natural healing processes, promoting healthy nail growth and reducing the risk of further complications.

  • Keep nails clean and dry to prevent fungal growth
  • Wear breathable shoes and change socks regularly
  • Avoid sharing personal care items to reduce the risk of infection

Prevention and Self-Care Strategies for Healthy Nails

In addition to homeopathic treatment, there are several self-care strategies that can help support healthy nail growth and prevent onychomycosis. These include keeping nails trimmed and clean, avoiding harsh chemicals, and wearing protective gloves when engaging in activities that expose hands to water or harsh substances. By combining these strategies with homeopathic care, individuals can take a proactive approach to maintaining healthy nails and overall well-being.

  • Trim nails straight across to prevent ingrown nails
  • Avoid using nail polish removers that contain harsh chemicals
  • Wear gloves when washing dishes or engaging in other water-intensive activities
