Understanding Stye and Chalazion: Causes and Symptoms

A stye is a small, usually painful, bump on the outside of the eyelid, while a chalazion is a larger, often painless, lump on the inside of the eyelid. Both conditions can be caused by blocked oil glands or allergic reactions.

In Varthur and nearby areas like Whitefield and Gunjur, people may be more prone to eye allergies due to the changing weather and increased pollen counts.

  • Increased tearing or discharge
