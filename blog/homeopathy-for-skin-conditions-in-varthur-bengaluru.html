Understanding the Impact of Monsoon Water Quality on Skin Conditions

The monsoon season in Varthur, Bengaluru, brings relief from the summer heat, but it also poses a significant threat to skin health. The increased moisture in the air and changes in water quality can exacerbate existing skin conditions, such as acne, eczema, and dermatitis. Homeopathic treatment tailored to an individual's symptom pattern may help alleviate these issues.

  • Increased humidity can lead to clogged pores and acne
  • Changes in water quality can irritate skin and trigger allergic reactions
