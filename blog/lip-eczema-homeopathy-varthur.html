Understanding Lip Eczema and Its Triggers

Lip eczema, also known as eczematous cheilitis, is a common condition characterized by inflammation, dryness, and cracking of the lips. During the monsoon season, the cool and humid weather can trigger or worsen lip eczema in people prone to the condition. Factors such as wind, cold temperatures, and dry air can strip the lips of their natural moisture, leading to dryness and irritation.

  • Wind and cold temperatures
  • Dry air and low humidity
  • Irritating substances like fragrances or dyes in lip care products

Practical Tips for Managing Lip Eczema at Home

While homeopathic treatment can provide relief from lip eczema symptoms, there are also practical steps you can take at home to manage the condition. Applying a gentle, fragrance-free lip balm regularly can help lock in moisture and protect the lips from the elements. Avoiding irritants like harsh soaps or exfoliating products can also reduce the risk of flare-ups.
