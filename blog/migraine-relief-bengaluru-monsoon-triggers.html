Understanding Vestibular Migraines and Monsoon Triggers

Vestibular migraines are a type of migraine that affects the inner ear and balance system, causing symptoms such as vertigo, dizziness, and nausea. For people living in Bengaluru, the monsoon season can be a particularly challenging time, as the changes in weather and air pressure can trigger these migraines.
