Understanding Monsoon Noise Pollution's Impact on Children's Cognitive Development

Research suggests that prolonged exposure to noise pollution, particularly during monsoon season, can have detrimental effects on children's cognitive development, including decreased attention span, memory, and learning abilities. Furthermore, the stress caused by noise pollution can lead to emotional disturbances, such as anxiety and irritability, in kids. Homeopathic approaches can help alleviate these issues by promoting relaxation, reducing stress, and supporting cognitive function.
