Migraine Relief in Bengaluru: TMJ Disorders

By Dr. Nafia M · 17 Aug 2026 · 4 min read

As the monsoon season sets in Bengaluru, some individuals may experience a surge in migraine episodes, often triggered by temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders. TMJ disorders can cause significant discomfort and pain, radiating to the jaw, face, and even triggering migraine headaches. At Dr. Nafia's Homoeopathic Medical Centre in Varthur, we understand the importance of addressing the underlying causes of migraine and offer homeopathic treatment tailored to individual needs.

Understanding the Connection Between TMJ Disorders and Migraine Episodes

Research suggests that there is a significant link between TMJ disorders and migraine episodes. The TMJ is a complex joint that plays a crucial role in our daily activities, such as chewing, speaking, and even breathing. When the TMJ is affected, it can lead to a cascade of symptoms, including jaw pain, clicking or locking of the jaw, and even migraine headaches.

In Bengaluru, the monsoon season can exacerbate TMJ disorders, leading to increased muscle tension and inflammation in the jaw and face. This, in turn, can trigger migraine episodes, making it essential to address the underlying causes of TMJ disorders to manage migraine symptoms effectively.

  • TMJ disorders can cause jaw pain, clicking or locking of the jaw, and facial pain
  • Monsoon season in Bengaluru can exacerbate TMJ disorders, leading to increased muscle tension and inflammation
  • Homeopathic treatment can help alleviate TMJ symptoms and reduce migraine episodes
