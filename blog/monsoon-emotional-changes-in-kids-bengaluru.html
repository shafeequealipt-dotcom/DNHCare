Understanding Monsoon-Related Emotional Changes in Kids

The monsoon season can be a time of significant change for children, with the rainy weather and increased humidity affecting their mood and behavior. Some kids may become more clingy or demanding, while others may become more withdrawn or irritable. Homeopathy recognizes that each child is unique and may respond differently to the same environmental stimuli. By understanding the individual child's emotional profile and attachment style, homeopathic treatment can be tailored to support their emotional regulation and parent-child bonding.
