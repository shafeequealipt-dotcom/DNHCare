Monsoon Sensory Overload in Kids Bengaluru

By Dr. Nafia M· 2026-09-10· 4 min read

As the monsoon season sets in Bengaluru, many children experience sensory overload due to the changed environment and weather conditions. For sensitive children, the loud thunder, flashes of lightning, and heavy rainfall can be particularly overwhelming. At Dr. Nafia's Homoeopathic Medical Centre in Varthur, we understand the importance of creating a calming environment and routine to help your child navigate this challenging time. In this article, we will explore the concept of sensory overload in children, its effects, and how homeopathic treatment can support your child's well-being during the monsoon season in Bengaluru.

Understanding Sensory Overload in Children During Monsoon Season

Sensory overload occurs when a child's senses are overwhelmed by their environment, leading to feelings of anxiety, stress, and discomfort. During the monsoon season in Bengaluru, the constant sound of rain, thunder, and lightning can be particularly triggering for sensitive children. Additionally, the changed weather conditions can disrupt routines, leading to further stress and anxiety.

  • Increased noise levels from thunder and heavy rainfall
  • Disrupted routines due to power outages or flooding
  • Changes in temperature and humidity

Creating Calming Environments for Sensitive Children in Varthur

Creating a calming environment is crucial for sensitive children during the monsoon season. This can be achieved by reducing noise levels, maintaining a consistent temperature, and promoting relaxation techniques such as deep breathing, meditation, or yoga. At Dr. Nafia's Homoeopathic Medical Centre, our team works with parents to develop personalized strategies for creating a calming environment tailored to their child's unique needs.

  • Use earplugs or headphones to reduce noise levels
  • Maintain a consistent temperature and humidity level
  • Encourage relaxation techniques such as deep breathing or meditation
