Understanding Monsoon-Induced Changes in Pollen Allergy Patterns

Furthermore, the urban bee population plays a crucial role in pollen distribution, and their activities during the monsoon can significantly impact allergy patterns. By understanding these factors, individuals can better prepare themselves for the allergy season.
