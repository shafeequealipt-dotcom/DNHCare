As the monsoon season sets in Bengaluru, the urban bee population undergoes significant changes, impacting pollen allergy patterns in the city. For individuals suffering from pollen allergy in Bengaluru, this can mean a surge in uncomfortable symptoms like congestion, sneezing, and itchy eyes. Homeopathy offers a holistic approach to addressing these allergies, focusing on individualized treatment and promoting overall respiratory wellness.

Understanding Monsoon-Induced Changes in Pollen Allergy Patterns

The monsoon season in Bengaluru brings about a unique set of challenges for individuals with pollen allergies. The changes in temperature and humidity during this time can lead to an increase in pollen counts, exacerbating symptoms. Furthermore, the urban bee population plays a crucial role in pollen distribution, and their activities during the monsoon can significantly impact allergy patterns. By understanding these factors, individuals can better prepare themselves for the allergy season.
