Understanding Atopic Dermatitis and Its Triggers

Atopic dermatitis, also known as eczema, is a chronic skin condition characterized by dry, itchy, and inflamed skin. It can be triggered by a variety of factors, including genetic predisposition, environmental allergens, and immune system dysfunction. In Bengaluru, the monsoon season introduces additional challenges, such as increased humidity and the presence of waterborne pathogens, which can exacerbate atopic dermatitis symptoms.

  • Genetic predisposition
  • Environmental allergens
  • Immune system dysfunction
  • Monsoon-induced humidity and waterborne pathogens

Prevention and Management Strategies for Atopic Dermatitis in Bengaluru

To prevent and manage atopic dermatitis during the monsoon season, it is essential to maintain good skin care habits, such as keeping the skin clean and moisturized. Additionally, avoiding exposure to known allergens and irritants, and managing stress through techniques like meditation or yoga, can help reduce the risk of flare-ups.
