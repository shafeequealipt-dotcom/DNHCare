Understanding Asthma Triggers in Festival Season

Asthma triggers can vary from person to person, but common culprits include smoke, pollution, and changes in weather. During festival seasons in Bengaluru, the air is often filled with smoke from fireworks and other celebrations, which can irritate the lungs and trigger asthma attacks. Families in Whitefield and Gunjur, nearby areas to Varthur, often face similar challenges due to the proximity of these locations.

Individuals with asthma should be aware of their surroundings and take necessary precautions to minimize exposure to triggers. This might include staying indoors when possible, using air purifiers, and avoiding areas with high smoke concentrations.

  • Stay indoors during peak smoke hours
  • Use air purifiers to clean the air
  • Avoid areas with high smoke concentrations
