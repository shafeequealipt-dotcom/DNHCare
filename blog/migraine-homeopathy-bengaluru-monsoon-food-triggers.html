By Dr. Nafia M· 2026-09-20· 4 min read

As the monsoon season sets in Bengaluru, the city's food scene undergoes a significant transformation, with an abundance of fresh, seasonal fruits and vegetables becoming available. However, for some individuals, this change in diet may trigger migraine episodes. At Dr. Nafia's Homoeopathic Medical Centre in Varthur, we've seen how an individually selected remedy can support migraine sufferers in managing their symptoms. In this article, we'll explore the connection between monsoon-induced changes in Bengaluru's food scene and migraine episodes, and discuss homeopathic relief strategies that may help alleviate symptoms.

Understanding Monsoon Food Triggers for Migraine in Bengaluru

The monsoon season in Bengaluru brings with it a plethora of fresh, seasonal fruits and vegetables, which can be a double-edged sword for migraine sufferers. On one hand, a diet rich in whole, nutrient-dense foods can help support overall health and wellbeing. On the other hand, certain foods may trigger migraine episodes in susceptible individuals. Common monsoon food triggers for migraine in Bengaluru include citrus fruits, tomatoes, and fermented foods.

  • Citrus fruits like oranges and lemons
  • Tomatoes and tomato-based products
  • Fermented foods like idlis and dosas
