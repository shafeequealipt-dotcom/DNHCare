Understanding the Connection Between Migraine and Vertigo

Migraine and vertigo are two distinct conditions that can be interconnected, especially during the monsoon season. Migraine is a neurological condition characterized by intense headaches, often accompanied by sensitivity to light and sound. Vertigo, on the other hand, is a type of dizziness that creates a false sense of spinning or movement. In some cases, vertigo can be a symptom of migraine, or the two conditions can co-occur due to shared underlying factors, such as inner ear balance issues or neurological sensitivities.
