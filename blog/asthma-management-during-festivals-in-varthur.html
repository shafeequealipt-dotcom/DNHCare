Understanding Asthma Triggers During Festival Season

The festival season in Bengaluru brings with it a mix of excitement and concern for asthma sufferers. Poor air quality, due to increased pollution from fireworks and other festivities, can trigger asthma attacks. It's essential to be aware of these triggers and take proactive steps to manage them. Homeopathic treatment can play a significant role in supporting your respiratory health during this time.

  • Avoid exposure to pollutants by staying indoors when possible
  • Use air purifiers to improve indoor air quality
  • Keep your home clean to reduce dust and other allergens
