Understanding Sinusitis and Nasal Polyps

Sinusitis is a common condition characterized by inflammation of the sinuses, often caused by allergies, infections, or environmental factors. Nasal polyps, which are growths that occur on the lining of the nasal passages, can also contribute to sinusitis symptoms. Homeopathic treatment may help alleviate congestion, pain, and pressure associated with these conditions.

  • Symptoms of sinusitis may include facial pain, headache, and nasal congestion
  • Nasal polyps can cause loss of smell, nasal discharge, and difficulty breathing
