Understanding Dry Skin on Elbows and Knees

Dry skin on the elbows and knees can be caused by a combination of factors, including cold weather, low humidity, and harsh soaps or cleansers. In Bengaluru's cool transition season, it's common for skin to become dry and rough, especially in areas that are prone to friction and pressure. Homeopathic remedies can help to nourish and moisturize the skin, reducing dryness and discomfort.
