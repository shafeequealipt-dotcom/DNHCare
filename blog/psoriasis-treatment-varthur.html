Understanding Psoriasis and Its Triggers

Psoriasis is a chronic skin condition characterized by thick, scaly patches on the skin, often accompanied by inflammation and discomfort. While the exact cause of psoriasis is unknown, various triggers can exacerbate symptoms, including stress, certain medications, and environmental factors like pollution and smoke from festivals.

In areas like Varthur, where festivals are an integral part of the culture, managing psoriasis requires a comprehensive approach that considers these unique environmental challenges.

  • Avoiding or minimizing exposure to festival smoke
  • Maintaining a healthy diet rich in omega-3 fatty acids and antioxidants
  • Staying hydrated to help flush out toxins
