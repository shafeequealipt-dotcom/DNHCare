Understanding PCOS and Its Symptoms

PCOS is a common endocrine disorder affecting women of reproductive age, characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, weight gain, acne, and excess hair growth. The condition is often associated with insulin resistance, which can increase the risk of developing type 2 diabetes and other metabolic disorders. During the monsoon season, hormonal fluctuations can worsen PCOS symptoms, making it essential to seek professional help for effective management.

Homeopathic treatment for PCOS in Bengaluru takes into account the individual's overall health, lifestyle, and symptom pattern. By addressing the underlying causes of the condition, homeopathy may help alleviate symptoms and improve quality of life.
