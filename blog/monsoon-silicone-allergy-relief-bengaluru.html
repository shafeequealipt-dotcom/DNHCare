Understanding Monsoon-Triggered Silicone Allergy

The monsoon season in Bengaluru can exacerbate silicone allergy symptoms, causing skin irritation, redness, and itchiness. This is often due to the increased humidity and moisture in the air, which can react with the silicone-based products used in cosmetics and personal care. As a result, individuals with silicone allergy may experience a worsening of their symptoms during this time.

It's crucial to recognize the signs of silicone allergy, including skin rashes, hives, and itching, to seek appropriate treatment. Homeopathic treatment, in particular, may offer a gentle and effective approach to managing these symptoms, without the need for harsh chemicals or medications.
