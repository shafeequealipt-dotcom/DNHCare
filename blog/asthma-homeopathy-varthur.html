Common questions

Can homeopathy replace inhalers for asthma management?

No, homeopathy is meant to complement, not replace, conventional asthma treatment. It's essential to continue using inhalers as prescribed by your doctor while exploring homeopathic options under professional guidance.

How does air quality affect asthma, and what can I do?

Poor air quality can trigger asthma attacks. Staying informed about local air quality, using air purifiers, and minimizing outdoor activities when pollution levels are high can help manage symptoms.

Is homeopathic treatment for asthma suitable for children?

Yes, homeopathy can be a gentle and non-invasive approach for children with asthma. It's crucial to consult with a qualified homeopathic doctor who can assess your child's specific needs and develop an appropriate treatment plan.
