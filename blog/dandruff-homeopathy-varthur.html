Understanding Dandruff and Its Symptoms

Dandruff is a common condition characterized by flakes of dead skin on the scalp, often accompanied by itchiness and redness. It can be triggered by various factors, including weather changes, stress, and individual sensitivity to hair care products. In Bengaluru's cool transition, the dry air may exacerbate dandruff symptoms, making it essential to find an effective treatment approach.

  • Flakes of dead skin on the scalp
  • Itchiness and redness
  • Triggered by weather changes, stress, or hair care products
