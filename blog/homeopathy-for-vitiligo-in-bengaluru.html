Understanding Vitiligo and Its Impact on Skin Health

Vitiligo is a chronic skin condition characterized by the loss of pigment-producing cells, resulting in white patches on the skin. In Bengaluru's humid climate, these patches can be more noticeable, affecting an individual's self-esteem and overall well-being. Homeopathic treatment may help alleviate symptoms and support skin health.

  • Homeopathy considers the physical, emotional, and genetic factors contributing to vitiligo
  • An individually selected remedy may help manage symptoms and promote repigmentation
