Understanding Vitiligo and Its Impact on Skin Health

Vitiligo is a chronic skin condition characterized by the loss of pigment-producing cells, resulting in white patches on the skin. In Bengaluru's humid climate, these patches can be more noticeable, affecting an individual's self-esteem and overall well-being.
