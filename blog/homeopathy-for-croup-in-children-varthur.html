Understanding Croup and Its Triggers in Children

Croup is a respiratory condition that affects children, typically under the age of five, and is often triggered by viral infections, allergies, or environmental factors like pollution and dust. During the monsoon season, the cool and humid weather can exacerbate croup symptoms, making it essential for parents to be aware of the signs and seek medical attention if necessary. Prevention Strategies for a Healthy Monsoon Season

While homeopathy can help alleviate croup symptoms, prevention is key to keeping your child healthy during the monsoon season. By maintaining good hygiene, avoiding exposure to pollutants and allergens, and supporting your child's immune system with a balanced diet and regular exercise, you can reduce the risk of croup and other illnesses.
