PCOS Weight Gain in Varthur: Managing Cravings

By Dr. Nafia M · 14 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

For women with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) in Varthur, Bengaluru, the festival season can be challenging, especially when it comes to managing weight gain and cravings. As the weather transitions to cooler temperatures, it's essential to focus on hormonal balance and healthy lifestyle choices to support overall well-being. Homeopathic treatment, tailored to individual needs, may help alleviate symptoms and support weight management.

Understanding PCOS and Weight Gain in Varthur

PCOS is a hormonal disorder that affects women of reproductive age, causing a range of symptoms including weight gain, acne, and irregular menstrual cycles. In Varthur, women with PCOS may experience increased cravings for sweet or high-carbohydrate foods, especially during festivals, which can exacerbate weight gain. A homeopathic approach focuses on individualized treatment, considering the unique symptom pattern and health history of each woman.

  • Identify and avoid trigger foods that can worsen cravings and weight gain
  • Incorporate physical activity, such as yoga or walking, to support hormonal balance and weight management
  • Get enough sleep and practice stress-reducing techniques, like meditation or deep breathing

Managing Cravings and Hormonal Balance during Festivals

Festivals in Bengaluru, like Diwali or Navratri, often involve traditional sweets and high-calorie foods, making it challenging for women with PCOS to stick to a healthy diet. By being mindful of portion sizes and choosing nutrient-dense foods, women can enjoy the festivities while minimizing the risk of weight gain. Homeopathic treatment, combined with lifestyle changes, may help reduce cravings and support hormonal balance.

  • Plan ahead and prepare healthy alternatives to traditional festival foods
  • Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water and limiting sugary drinks
  • Seek support from family and friends to maintain a healthy lifestyle during festivals
