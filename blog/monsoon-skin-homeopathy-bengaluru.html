Understanding the Impact of Monsoon on Skin Microbiome

The monsoon season in Bengaluru brings with it a unique set of challenges for our skin. The increased humidity can lead to an overgrowth of certain microorganisms on the skin, causing issues like acne, fungal infections, and itchiness. Homeopathic treatment tailored to your skin's symptom pattern can help alleviate these issues and restore balance to the skin microbiome.

  • Keep your skin clean and dry to prevent bacterial and fungal growth
  • Use gentle, fragrance-free products suitable for your skin type
  • Stay hydrated to help your skin regulate its natural barrier function
