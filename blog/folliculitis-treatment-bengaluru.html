Folliculitis Treatment in Bengaluru

By Dr. Nafia M· 2026-08-03· 4 min read

As the monsoon season sets in Bengaluru, many of us struggle with folliculitis, a common skin condition characterized by ingrown hairs and infected hair follicles. Folliculitis treatment in Bengaluru can be challenging due to the city's humid climate, which exacerbates the condition. At Dr. Nafia's Homoeopathic Medical Centre in Varthur, we offer individualized homeopathic remedies and self-care tips to help manage folliculitis and promote healthy skin.

Understanding Folliculitis and Its Causes

Folliculitis is a skin condition that occurs when the hair follicles become inflamed, often due to bacterial or fungal infections. In Bengaluru's humid climate, the risk of folliculitis increases, especially during the monsoon season. Ingrown hairs, shaving, and clogged pores can also contribute to the development of folliculitis.

Homeopathic treatment for folliculitis focuses on addressing the underlying causes of the condition, rather than just treating the symptoms. By identifying the individual's unique symptom pattern, our homeopathic doctor can prescribe an individually selected remedy to support the body's natural healing process.

Homeopathic Remedies and Self-Care Tips for Managing Folliculitis

In addition to homeopathic remedies, there are several self-care tips that can help manage folliculitis. Keeping the skin clean and dry, avoiding tight clothing, and using gentle skin care products can help reduce the risk of infection. Exfoliating regularly and avoiding shaving or waxing can also help prevent ingrown hairs.

  • Avoid tight clothing and harsh skin care products
  • Exfoliate regularly to prevent ingrown hairs

Common questions

What are the symptoms of folliculitis?

Common symptoms of folliculitis include redness, itching, and pus-filled bumps on the skin, often accompanied by ingrown hairs or infected hair follicles.

Can homeopathy help with folliculitis?

Yes, homeopathy can help manage folliculitis by addressing the underlying causes of the condition and promoting the body's natural healing process.

How can I prevent folliculitis in Bengaluru's humid climate?

To prevent folliculitis, keep your skin clean and dry, avoid tight clothing, and use gentle skin care products. Exfoliating regularly and avoiding shaving or waxing can also help reduce the risk of ingrown hairs and infected hair follicles.
