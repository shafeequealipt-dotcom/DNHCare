Understanding Monsoon Allergies in Bengaluru

The monsoon season in Bengaluru brings relief from the summer heat, but it also poses a threat to human health due to the increased presence of microplastics in the city's water sources. Microplastics can enter the water supply through various means, including sewage, industrial waste, and plastic pollution. When ingested, these microplastics can trigger allergic reactions, ranging from mild symptoms like skin rashes and itching to more severe conditions like respiratory issues and digestive problems.

  • Microplastic exposure can also lead to digestive problems and other health concerns

Practical Tips for Reducing Microplastic Exposure in Bengaluru

While homeopathic treatment can help alleviate symptoms, it is also essential to take practical steps to reduce microplastic exposure in Bengaluru. This can include using water filters, avoiding plastic products, and supporting initiatives that promote water conservation and pollution reduction. By taking a proactive approach to reducing microplastic exposure, individuals can help minimize their risk of developing monsoon allergies and support the health of their community.
