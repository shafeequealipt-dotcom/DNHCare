Understanding the Gut-Brain Axis and Its Role in Allergies

The gut-brain axis refers to the bidirectional communication network between the gut microbiome and the central nervous system. During the monsoons, changes in temperature, humidity, and air quality can disrupt this delicate balance, leading to an increased risk of allergic reactions. Homeopathy recognizes the importance of this axis in maintaining immune health and offers tailored treatments to restore balance and promote resilience.

  • Identify and avoid common allergens
  • Maintain a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains
  • Stay hydrated to help flush out toxins
