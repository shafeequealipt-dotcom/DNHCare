Understanding Dermatographic Urticaria and Its Triggers

Dermatographic urticaria, also known as skin writing, is a condition where the skin reacts to pressure or scratching by raising red, itchy welts. In Bengaluru's humid climate, especially during the monsoon season, these symptoms can worsen due to increased moisture and temperature fluctuations. Homeopathic treatment for dermatographic urticaria focuses on addressing the underlying causes of the condition, rather than just its symptoms.
